02 — About the archive

I collect the moments that rearranged me.

I began keeping this archive because certain stories were doing more than entertaining me. They were handing me language for things I could not yet explain: the shape of grief, the cost of ambition, the strange courage required to stay soft in a hard world.

Some of these are masterpieces. Some are simply mine. What connects them is not a genre or a rating, but a residue — a question that stayed after the credits, a character whose voice I still hear when I have to make a decision.

I believe fiction is not an escape from real life. It is a rehearsal space for it. The stories we return to quietly teach us what to notice, what to forgive, and who we might become.

“A story is a machine for feeling another life from the inside.”

    Attention is a form of love.
