02 — About the archive

I collect the moments that rearranged me.

I’m Janne Denzelle Tagupa, and I created this space to keep the stories that left something behind. Some made me think differently, some inspired me, and others simply gave me a feeling I couldn't quite put into words.

As someone who appreciates creativity, visual storytelling, and the art of filmmaking, I believe movies are more than just images on a screen. Behind every scene, character, and line of dialogue is an idea waiting to be understood. Sometimes, a story can make us see things from a different perspective, remind us of something we've forgotten, or teach us something about ourselves.

This archive is a collection of those experiences. The films I enjoyed, the characters I connected with, the scenes I remember, and the lessons I carry with me even after the credits roll.

“Every story leaves something behind. Sometimes, it becomes part of who we are”

  1. 01

    Stories shape the way we see the world.

    Every film introduces a different perspective. Through stories, I get to understand experiences beyond my own.

  2. 02

    Every detail has a meaning.

    A character's decision, a scene's composition, or even a single line can communicate something deeper than what we initially see.

  3. 03

    Inspiration can come from anywhere.

    Movies remind me that creativity has no limits. They inspire the way I think, create, and express my own ideas.
